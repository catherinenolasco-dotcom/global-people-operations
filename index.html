Case studies

How the work actually went.

Ridecell first, then Prezent AI.

People Operations built from zero

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

Challenge

Ridecell had no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount lived in spreadsheets, and there was no tracked channel for employee questions.

What I did

Built a 40+ procedure SOP library, launched ASKPeople on Jira and Confluence with tiered SLAs, and made Sapling the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and Zapier connections.

Result

Scaled from 3 to 5 countries with 6 consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits, approval cycles cut from 1-2 days to 2-3 hours, and 5-10 tickets a day resolved in 24-48 hours.

What I would do differently

I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

One approval path for every HRIS change

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process that created audit risk, and there was early pushback that a form would be too process-heavy.

What I did

Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form requiring documentation and approval before any HRIS change, plus Notion New Hire and Offboarding workflows.

Result

100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed, and preboarding within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently

I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

A reorganization with cost visibility

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

A reorganization affecting 18 employees across the US and India needed accurate severance and COBRA costs, and leadership had never seen reduction-in-force cost up front.

What I did

Built AI-enabled severance and cost modeling with Claude and Gemini that calculated severance, COBRA cost and COBRA months owed and drafted agreements, tracked on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result

First-time RIF cost visibility, and agreement prep cut from 20 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total.

What I would do differently

I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

How I would start.

A proposed plan built around the responsibilities of a global People leader.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

  • Meet leaders across regions and learn how each team hires, pays and supports people.
  • Map entities, systems of record, Employer of Record arrangements and open compliance risks.
  • Review handbooks, SOPs and the audit calendar to see what exists today.
Days 31 to 60

Design the first moves

  • Pick two or three high-impact fixes, such as one approval path for HRIS changes.
  • Draft the global operating rhythm: monthly planning, ticketing with tiered SLAs, and clear owners.
  • Pilot new workflows with one team before rolling them out.
Days 61 to 90

Ship and measure

  • Launch the first workflows and track adoption, response times and audit trail coverage.
  • Run a focused compliance check on the highest-risk areas.
  • Share a simple People dashboard with leadership.
By month 6

The outcome

  • A documented, owned People Ops operating model that scales across regions.
  • AI handling the repeatable work so the team spends time on the conversations that need judgment and empathy.
  • A People roadmap agreed with the leadership team.

This is a starting point. I would adjust it after the first conversations with leadership.
